> **Pre-release.** Your PC and desk in 3D: a new My setup page, and the Lighting page's
> preview in 3D.

## New

- **My setup**, a new page: your PC and desk in 3D, live.
  - The case with everything LumaBridge found inside: the motherboard (with its RGB), the
    CPU cooler, the memory in its slots, the graphics card, drives, the PSU shroud and the
    fans. On the desk: the monitor, the keyboard (lit key by key), the mouse, a headset if
    you have one, and other lit devices as light bars.
  - LEDs show their live colors. Fans spin with the speeds the sensors report (scaled so the
    blades stay readable; hover a fan for its real RPM), and the airflow runs from the
    intakes through the case to the exhausts, cool to warm.
  - Drag things around on the desk (the case's parts always stay in the case), turn the case,
    turn and zoom the view.
  - **Correct the fans:** LumaBridge guesses where your fans are (6 fans: 3 in front, 1 at the
    back, 2 on top). Click Edit fans, then click a slot to make it empty, a fan or an RGB fan,
    and set which way each position blows. It tells you whether the case runs positive or
    negative pressure.
  - Hover any part for what it is and its live readings (load, temperature, clock, fan
    speed). Parts without RGB say so.
- **The Lighting page's preview is 3D** too (and the setup guide's): in Manual mode, click a
  lit part to edit its lighting; parts without RGB can't be picked and say "No RGB".

Fan speeds need Hardware access (Devices page); without it the fans spin at a guess.
