> **Pre-release.** A hand cursor on every button, and a smoother sidebar.

## Fixed

- **Hand cursor everywhere:** every plain button in the app (Settings, Integrations, device
  pages, dialogs - about 50 of them) now shows a hand cursor on hover, not just the custom
  tiles and switches from the last round. Checked in the running app (built and launched on
  Windows this round, not just compiled).
- **Sidebar collapse arrow:** was pointing the wrong way (right when expanded, left when
  collapsed - backwards). Fixed, and confirmed correct in the running app.

## New

- **Smooth sidebar transition:** collapsing or expanding the sidebar now eases its width
  in and out instead of snapping, matching the toggle switches elsewhere in the app. The
  eased-width behavior is unverified in the running app (only the fixed arrow direction and
  default state were screenshotted this round); it follows the same, already-working
  animation pattern used by the toggle switches.
