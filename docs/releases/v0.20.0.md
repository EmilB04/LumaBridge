> **Pre-release.** 2D or 3D on the Lighting page, your cooler and graphics card on Devices,
> and NZXT Krakens found by name.

## New

- **2D or 3D:** the Lighting page's Your setup card has a 2D / 3D switch (top right). 2D is
  the flat view from before: your lit devices, dragged where you like, clicked to give one
  its own lighting. The choice is remembered.
- **Devices lists your AIO cooler** when one is found, with its own page: whether LumaBridge
  is reading it, its readings (NZXT Kraken), and a button to show it in My setup.
- **Devices lists your graphics card** too, even with no RGB: its page has its live load,
  temperature, fan, power, clocks and memory (NVIDIA cards), and says it has no lighting
  LumaBridge can control.
- **NZXT Krakens found by name:** any USB device calling itself "Kraken" is taken as one,
  whatever its product number. The log records what NZXT has on USB and which cooler was
  recognised.

## Changed

- **Direction** (which way moving effects run) is now only on each device's own page
  (Lighting > the device), not in a card for all of them on the main Lighting page.
