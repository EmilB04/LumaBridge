> **Pre-release.** The ROG Azoth's OLED screen can be controlled from LumaBridge (test), memory is one entry on the Devices page, and the side menu has a new look.

## New

- **ROG Azoth OLED display (test):** **Devices > ASUS ROG Azoth > OLED display** can switch
  the screen on or off, set its brightness, and show one of its six built-in animations or a
  12/24-hour clock from your PC's time, by cable or through the Omni receiver. It's off by
  default: the screen keeps what Armoury Crate put there until you choose LumaBridge. Each
  command waits for the keyboard to confirm it, and the status says when it did. Screen
  power and brightness were confirmed on a real keyboard; the animations and clock still
  need checking.
- **Live screen preview:** the OLED card shows a 256 x 64 mockup of the screen with your
  choice: the clock, a stand-in for the chosen animation, the brightness, or off.
- **Banner for Armoury Crate:** type a text, pick a size and black or white background, see
  it in a preview, and export a 256 x 64 BMP ready for Armoury Crate's image upload.

## Changed

- **Memory on the Devices page:** all RAM sticks are one **Memory (RAM)** entry, with a
  short summary ("3 sticks, 32 GB @ 3600 MT/s"). Its page lists each stick by slot, size and
  speed; click one for its details. The lighting settings appear there only when
  LumaBridge can light the sticks.
- **Device and game lists:** the whole row lights up under the mouse and opens on a click,
  however tall it is. Long text is cut with "..." and shown in full on hover, and the
  status pills line up with the text beside them.
- **Side menu:** a larger collapse button with a panel icon, the live status and version in
  their own card at the bottom, and the pages scroll above it on short windows (the card
  keeps its size).
- **ROG Azoth status:** follows the keyboard being plugged in or out, prefers the cable
  over the Omni receiver, and counts the receiver as connected only once the keyboard
  answers.
