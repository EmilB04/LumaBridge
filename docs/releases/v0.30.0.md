> **Pre-release.** Five more games light up through their own data, the 2D Lighting view shows the same devices as 3D, and Devices gets an All hardware inventory.

## New

- **More built-in game lighting:** BeamNG.drive (OutGauge), DiRT Rally and DiRT Rally 2.0
  (UDP telemetry), Automobilista 2 and Project CARS 2 (UDP), X-Plane 11 and 12 (UDP data
  output) and Elite Dangerous (its own Status.json, nothing to set up). Racing games give
  rev lights with a red flash at the limit; X-Plane follows G-load; Elite follows your ship's
  state (supercruise, hardpoints, FSD jump, low fuel, danger, interdiction, overheating).
  Each has a tile on the Games List with its setup steps and a saved port. These feeds are
  written to the games' public formats and are not yet checked against the running games.
- **All hardware:** a new tab under Devices lists everything Windows reports as connected
  (USB, Bluetooth, PCI, storage, audio, network, monitors, system devices), plus your
  motherboard and memory modules from firmware. Search, filter by category, rescan, and
  click a device for its details. Problems Windows reports are flagged.

## Changed

- **Devices tabs:** the tab bar is now the same pill switch used elsewhere, with hover
  and hand-cursor feedback.
- **2D Lighting view:** shows every device the 3D view shows (graphics card, headset,
  DualSense, other lit devices and plain keyboard or mouse), and the drawings are more
  detailed: DualSense, fans, board and RAM, mouse, keyboard and headsets.
- **Wording:** device types in the hardware list are always in English, whatever language
  Windows runs in.

## Fixed

- **2D and 3D showing different devices** on the Lighting page.
- **Overlapping elements** in the All hardware list on narrow windows.
