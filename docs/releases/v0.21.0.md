> **Pre-release.** Your Kraken's screen shows the liquid temperature in My setup, and the
> list of what LumaBridge found is grouped.

## New

- **The pump's screen** (NZXT Kraken with a screen) shows the liquid temperature in the
  middle of its ring, as NZXT's default screen does, live from the Kraken (the CPU's
  temperature if the Kraken isn't reporting).
- **What LumaBridge found** (My setup) is grouped: Processor and motherboard, Graphics,
  Cooling (your AIO by name, with its radiator, liquid temperature and pump speed, and the
  case fans), Storage, Monitors (each with its size, resolution and refresh rate) and On
  the desk.

## Fixed

- Selecting the case (or anything) drew an outline around every face of every part in it,
  so it looked X-rayed. It's now one outline around its outer edge; each monitor gets its
  own.
