> **Pre-release.** Graphs on the dashboard, and your power supply in Power and clocks.

## New

- **Graphs:** the dashboard's Processor, Graphics, Memory, Temperatures and Power and clocks
  cards show the last two minutes under their figure (load, memory used, CPU temperature,
  the PC's power draw). Hover a graph for the value at that moment. The **Graphs** switch
  next to Customize turns them off (remembered). They keep recording while you're on other
  pages.
- **Power supply:** Power and clocks now shows what the whole PC draws out of your power
  supply's rating, e.g. "342 W of 850 W", with a bar (amber from 70 %, red from 90 %) and
  a graph. The draw is the processor's and the graphics card's reported power plus an
  estimate for the parts that report none (motherboard, memory, drives, fans: about 35 W,
  5 W a drive, 2 W a fan), shown on its own line.
  - Corsair HX...i / RM...i and NZXT E power supplies are recognised over USB, with their
    rating.
  - For any other supply, enter its watts (on its label) in the card, once.
