> **Pre-release.** Lighting zones, direct Nollie hub support, more effects across the setup, customizable DualSense player lights and a chosen color when LumaBridge lets go.

## New

- **Lighting zones:** group devices into up to eight named zones, each with its own effect,
  colors, brightness, direction, travel and Manual or Auto mode. Auto zones can show your
  lighting, a rainbow or darkness between games. Devices outside zones follow Everything.
  Create a zone yourself or start with Inside the PC, Desk, or Keyboard and mouse. Delete
  a custom zone with the X on its button under Your zones; its devices return to Everything.
- **Nollie ARGB hubs:** direct USB lighting for Nollie 1, 8, 16 and 32 channel hubs, the
  28 series and Prism8. On Devices, say what is plugged into each channel: a strip or a
  chain of fans, with their LED counts. Test a channel to identify its wiring, then give
  channels or individual fans their own lighting. Unconfigured channels are left alone.
- **Nollie travel, sequences and gradients:** move effects fan by fan or smoothly LED by
  LED, fill and empty fans in order, bounce through them or stack up lights. Use gradients
  with up to six colors, still or flowing. Configured hubs participate in lighting zones
  and effects across the setup.
- **Fourteen effects across the setup:** Fill one by one, One at a time, Bounce, Rainbow
  by part, Lightning, Alternate, Ripple and Countdown treat each fan, strip, memory stick
  and device as a part. Fill, Center and Ignite each also offer a solid or pulsing finish.
  Their order follows the arrangement in Your setup; memory sticks can act separately
  or together.
- **Nollie boot lighting:** choose an effect and duration on the hub's Devices page, or
  try it with Play it now. It plays when LumaBridge starts lighting a configured hub,
  including after reconnect, then blends into normal lighting. The six solid/pulse
  reveals use the chosen duration and keep their boot colors independent of gradients.
  This is played by the app, rather than saved as a firmware power-on effect.
- **DualSense player lights:** customize the five white indicators below the touchpad.
  Follow the effect, stay steady, chase, breathe or blink; choose individual lights,
  speed and which effects use them. Moving effects also animate the lightbar's color.
- **A color when letting go:** under Settings -> When LumaBridge lets go, choose a final
  color for enabled lighting outputs on Stop lighting, exit or an individual handback.
  Disabled or already native outputs are excluded. A companion can reapply it after a
  crash or forced close; reopening cancels recovery. Killing the entire process tree or
  shutting down Windows also stops the companion, and other lighting software can
  replace the color afterward.

## Changed

- **One Lighting inspector:** Effect, Color, Motion, Devices and Games sit beside the live
  setup, with Your zones on the left in wide windows and compact zone buttons above in
  narrower ones. Existing individual device looks become zones automatically.
- **Center to pulse repeats:** ongoing lighting expands from the center, completes one
  gentle four-second pulse, retreats, pauses dark for a second and starts again. Boot
  playback reveals once and settles into the pulse before blending into normal lighting.
- **Smoother timing:** lighting outputs use a shared precise clock and paced frame timers,
  with shorter keep-alives for unchanged frames. RAM targets 30 frames a second; its
  actual bus transaction time and completed-frame count appear on the Memory page.
- **Rounded, animated popups:** menus, dropdowns, notifications and hover hints fade in
  and out, with rounded borders and padded hints. Closing menus release input immediately.
- **Hardware access update:** older helpers prompt for an update. The app, installed helper
  and installer now agree on task version 8, required for the RAM delivery improvements.

## Fixed

- **Effect editor crash:** selecting a setup reveal in the boot editor no longer indexes
  past its color labels. Effect metadata is shared and checked; duration-driven boot
  previews use the same timing as playback.
- **Incomplete RAM lighting:** frames write all four documented RGB groups, with coherent
  app/helper updates and acknowledgements after successful bus transfers. Failed transfers
  retry without consuming a frame; measured timing helps diagnose hardware limits.
- **Exit and restart hangs:** device workers receive stop requests before joins, blocked
  Windows I/O can be cancelled, GameSense owns its client threads, and network workers
  use deadlines. Intentional exits record clean shutdown before cleanup and cancel recovery.
- **Mixed zone modes:** Auto zones can request screen colors while Everything is Manual.
  Everything's idle handback keeps active zones running, and keyboard/mouse inactivity
  follows the game status of their own zone.
- **Settings and handoff reliability:** invalid numeric values are rejected, unrelated
  slider edits avoid repeated handoff-settings writes, and final-color delivery waits for
  successful-write acknowledgements within a deadline. Missing acknowledgements are logged.

## Validation

- The x64 Windows app and helper and the Win32 integration DLLs build with MinGW.
  The published package is built with MSVC by the release workflow.
- Linux core tests, Windows integration tests, helper-version and handback checks, and
  headless popup tests pass, covering effect boundaries, boot timing, saved settings, zone deletion,
  worker shutdown, delivery receipts, nested popups and rapid reopening.
- Checked the Lighting page, zone deletion, menus, dropdowns and hover hints in Wine.
  Full-app exit, restart and forced-exit recovery checks passed in an isolated profile.
- Nollie channels, individual fans, travel and sequences were previously confirmed on
  the owner's hub with three fans and a strip. Other hub models remain unverified.
- Physical RAM smoothness and stick order, DualSense indicator behavior and Bluetooth
  color retention, reconnects and sleep/wake still need observation. More crash diagnostics
  are included; the older terminate-only crash's exact cause remains unconfirmed.
