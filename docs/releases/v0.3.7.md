> **Pre-release.** Logitech mice (and other Logitech RGB gear) follow LumaBridge, and a
> read-only probe to bring ASUS ROG keyboards next.

## New

- **Logitech devices** (e.g. the G502 X Plus) show LumaBridge's lighting. LumaBridge uses
  Logitech's own LED SDK through G HUB, from its own process: nothing goes into a game,
  and G HUB does the device I/O. Devices show one color (the effect's first LED, animated).
  - LumaBridge hands them back to G HUB automatically whenever it isn't controlling the
    lights (Armoury Crate hand-back, Stop), and while a game lights Logitech gear itself
    (e.g. Battlefield 1 through G HUB, or a game through LumaBridge's Logitech proxy).
  - Devices page → **Keyboard & mouse** shows the state and has an on/off switch
    (`[App] LogitechDevices`).
- **ASUS ROG keyboards (Azoth): step one.** ASUS's Aura SDK crashes on the Azoth, so
  LumaBridge will drive it over USB like the motherboard. **`tools\device-probe.exe`**
  (also **Run the device probe** on the Devices page) lists the USB interfaces of ASUS and
  Logitech devices without sending them anything. That includes whether they speak the
  standard HID LampArray protocol behind Windows Dynamic Lighting. Its output
  (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaBridge\device-probe.txt`) decides how keyboard lighting is built.
- The dashboard's **Aura devices** card lists the Logitech devices too, and there are new
  mouse and keyboard icons.
