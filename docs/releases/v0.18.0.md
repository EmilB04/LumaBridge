> **Pre-release.** Your AIO cooler, recognised and read: LumaBridge now knows which AIO is
> plugged in, reads an NZXT Kraken's own status, and draws radiators and small screens as
> they really sit.

## New

- **AIO detection:** LumaBridge recognises NZXT Kraken (X, Z, Kraken and Kraken Elite,
  with or without a screen), Corsair iCUE and ASUS ROG Ryujin coolers on USB. Fans and
  cooling on My setup says which one it found ("Found on USB: ...") and offers to show it.
- **NZXT Kraken status:** with a Kraken plugged in, LumaBridge reads its liquid temperature,
  pump speed and (screen models) radiator fan speed straight from the cooler. They show on
  the dashboard (Fans, Temperatures), in My setup's tooltips, and the 3D pump spins at the
  real speed. Read only: NZXT CAM keeps the lighting and the pump screen.
- **Pump screen:** a Kraken with a screen shows a temperature ring on the pump head, as
  NZXT's default screen does (the liquid temperature when the Kraken reports it).
- **Small screens rest on the desk:** monitors under 16" (sensor panels, portable screens)
  sit on the desk leaning back, with no stand.

## Fixed

- A radiator that blows out now sits against the case panel with its fans inside the case,
  pulling air through it, as it's built in real cases.
