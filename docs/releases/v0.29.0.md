> **Pre-release.** More detailed 3D models, steadier rendering from different angles, and monitor size corrections.

## New

- **Monitor size corrections:** enter each monitor's actual diagonal in inches under
  **My setup → Monitors** when its reported size is wrong. Corrections are saved per monitor
  and update the size and placement in both 3D views. **Use reported size** restores automatic sizing.

## Changed

- **DualSense:** shaped two-tone shell and grips, touchpad, sticks, d-pad, face buttons,
  triggers and light strips showing the controller's live lightbar color. Drag and rotate
  it on the desk like the other devices.
- **Keyboard and mouse:** beveled keycaps with legends, the Azoth's OLED and control knob,
  and a curved mouse with separate buttons, grooved wheel, thumb rest and eight RGB zones.
- **Computer:** chassis rails, vents, glass and ports, with more motherboard, graphics card,
  cabling and cooler detail.

## Fixed

- **3D parts drawing through each other when the view turns:** a depth buffer keeps visible
  faces, edges, glass and glow in front of or behind the surrounding geometry. Near-camera
  clipping preserves visible portions of faces instead of dropping the whole face.
- **Clicking and moving in 3D:** click targets follow the visible surface, and models and
  labels update in the same frame as camera movement and dragging.
- **Mouse model:** RGB strips and button panels follow the curved shell without cutting
  through it; end caps close the gaps under the arch.
