> **Pre-release.** A notification center, a live controller page with button mapping, RAM lighting on Intel boards, Kraken lighting, and OpenRGB for many more devices.

## New

- **Notifications:** the bell at the top lists what needs your attention, most serious
  first: a lighting connection that stopped, a game port another program took, a game
  that sends nothing, a low controller battery, devices Windows reports a problem for. Each
  has a button that opens the right page and a cross to dismiss it; dismissed notices can
  be shown again, and come back by themselves if the problem returns.
- **Controller page:** a DualSense or DualShock 4 (USB or Bluetooth) shows its buttons,
  sticks, triggers and motion live, its battery, and how steadily it reports. An optional
  button mapping (off until you switch it on) makes buttons press keyboard keys or mouse
  buttons, and the right stick can move the mouse. DualShock 4 is untested on real
  hardware.
- **RAM lighting on Intel boards (experimental):** HyperX / Kingston FURY RGB DDR4 now
  also works on Intel I801 chipsets, through PawnIO's signed SmbusI801 module. Untested on
  real Intel hardware. Existing installs need **Hardware access > Set up again** once.
- **NZXT Kraken lighting (experimental):** X53/X63/X73 pump ring, logo and NZXT RGB
  accessories, and Z53/Z63/Z73 accessories, can follow LumaBridge. Off by default; switch
  it on on the cooler's page. Follows liquidctl's documented protocol; untested on those
  models. The LCD and pump/fan curves stay with NZXT CAM.

## Changed

- **OpenRGB:** every device its SDK server exposes can follow LumaBridge, with its own
  saved switch (identical RAM sticks or controllers are told apart). A working native
  connection still comes first; OpenRGB can stand in when one isn't working.
- **Discovery:** Windows lighting and OpenRGB devices show up on Devices even when their
  lighting control is off, grouped as fan and RGB controllers, memory, peripherals,
  cooling and graphics. Memory modules are listed by slot, maker and part number.

## Fixed

- **Windows lighting and OpenRGB switches** are saved by device identity, so two devices
  with the same name no longer share one setting. Older settings are kept.
