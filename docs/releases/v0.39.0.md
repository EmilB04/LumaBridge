> **Pre-release.** A taller Azoth audio EQ and a song progress bar.

## New

- **Song progress:** Song info on the Azoth now shows the elapsed time, a progress bar and
  the song length under the title and artist, when the player shares them with Windows.
  The time keeps counting between the player's updates and stops when paused.

## Changed

- **Taller EQ:** the Azoth audio EQ scales itself to the music, so quiet songs and a low
  Windows volume still fill the screen. High notes get a small lift; silence stays dark.
- **Song info layout:** title and artist are centered.

## Validation

- The x64 app builds and the portable tests pass, including new tests for the EQ scale
  and the progress bar.
- Checked by the owner on the original ROG Azoth by USB cable.
