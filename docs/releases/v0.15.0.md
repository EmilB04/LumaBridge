> **Pre-release.** A new dashboard, a setup step to pick your look, left / right direction and
> hand-back for each device, and built-in lighting for Microsoft Flight Simulator and DCS World.

## New

- **Setup: pick your look.** The setup guide ends with a choice of looks (gradients,
  rainbows, moving and calm effects), shown live on every device, and whether games light
  everything or your look always stays.
- **Dashboard.** Lighting and Game are always the top row: the running effect, how many
  devices are lit, brightness, and your games with built-in lighting (the others under "Also
  supported"). The other cards sit in Performance, Cooling, Devices and System sections, with
  two new cards: **Storage** (drives and their temperatures) and **Power and clocks**.
- **Direction for each device.** Left / right arrows everywhere a direction is set: in the
  effect settings, and per device on the Lighting page's new Direction card (right by default).
- **Hand a device back to its own app.** Next to "Same as all devices" and "Its own lighting":
  back to Armoury Crate (fans, motherboard, memory, keyboard), G HUB (mouse) or the device's
  own lighting. LumaBridge then leaves it alone, games included. The fans and the motherboard
  share one ASUS controller, so they go back together.
- **Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 / 2024)**, through SimConnect: the sky's color by time
  of day, your aircraft's beacon and strobe lights, stall and overspeed warnings, low fuel
  and crashes. Needs SimConnect.dll from Microsoft's free Flight Simulator SDK (its page says
  how).
- **DCS World**, through DCS's export script: the sky by the mission's time, three greens
  with the gear down, red under high G, and the master warning. Set up once on its page.
- **More games recognized:** Euro Truck Simulator 2, The Last of Us Part I and The Sims 4
  (Logitech LIGHTSYNC); Apex Legends, PUBG, Marvel Rivals and HITMAN (Razer Chroma); Destiny
  2, Rainbow Six Siege, Red Dead Redemption 2 and Call of Duty marked as having no lighting of
  their own; Wallpaper Engine is not a game.
- **Auto / Manual switch** redrawn, with a **?** that explains both modes.
- The Games List shows your games first, with built-in game lighting below.

## Fixed

- Rainbow wave and Gradient ran the opposite way to Comet; every moving effect now runs the
  way its direction says.
- The dashboard's device card now lists non-ASUS devices on PCs without Aura.

Flight Simulator and DCS World are not yet tried in a real flight - feedback welcome.
