> **Pre-release.** The ROG Azoth's keys and OLED follow LumaBridge's inactivity timeout, even when Armoury Crate has a shorter timer.

## Fixed

- **Azoth turning off too early:** LumaBridge overrides the keyboard's firmware sleep timer
  while controlling it, so Armoury Crate's shorter timeout no longer turns the keys and
  OLED off before LumaBridge's chosen delay.
- **OLED staying on after the keys fade out:** both outputs now turn off at LumaBridge's
  inactivity timeout. The next key press restores the lighting and display.
- **ASUS timer restoration:** the original firmware sleep timer is restored when the
  keyboard becomes idle or LumaBridge releases control. Device settings report a rejected
  timer override instead of silently leaving the shorter firmware timeout in effect.
