> **Pre-release.** Adds a hardware test tool; the app itself is unchanged from v0.2.2.

## New: `aura-usb-test.exe`

On current Armoury Crate versions ASUS's Aura SDK reports **no devices**, so LumaBridge
is moving to controlling the motherboard's **AURA LED Controller** (USB `0B05:1939`, e.g.
ROG STRIX B550-F) directly over USB. This tool checks that on your PC before the app
switches over.

1. Quit Armoury Crate (Task Manager → end `ArmouryCrate.exe`) so it doesn't repaint during
   the test.
2. In PowerShell in the LumaBridge folder:
   - `.\aura-usb-test.exe info`: firmware and how many ARGB headers and LEDs the
     controller reports
   - `.\aura-usb-test.exe scan`: lights channels 0–7 in red, one at a time; note what
     lights up for each
   - `.\aura-usb-test.exe color 00FF00`: everything green (`... color 00FF00 1` = channel 1 only)
3. Reopen Armoury Crate or reboot to restore your normal lighting. The tool never saves
   anything to the controller.
