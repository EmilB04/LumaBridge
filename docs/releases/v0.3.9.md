> **Pre-release.** The ASUS ROG Azoth now follows LumaBridge wirelessly too (experimental).

## New

- **ASUS ROG Azoth over wireless (experimental).** Through the ROG Omni receiver, the whole
  keyboard shows LumaBridge's color, just like over the cable. The command is the one
  Armoury Crate sends over 2.4 GHz, captured on your Azoth.
  - The cable is used when it's plugged in; otherwise LumaBridge finds the Omni receiver.
  - As before, LumaBridge **never** sends Armoury Crate's save command, so your saved
    lighting stays stored in the keyboard.
  - The setting is now just **Light the ROG Azoth** (Devices → **Keyboard & mouse**), still
    off by default. The card and the dashboard show whether it's wired or wireless.
  - Not yet: per-key colors, and handing back without a restart.
- Protocol notes: [docs/peripherals.md](https://github.com/EmilB04/LumaBridge/blob/main/docs/peripherals.md).
