> **Pre-release.** My setup, corrected and extended: your case the right way round, every
> monitor as Windows arranges them, things you can turn, and wind for airflow.

## New

- **Your monitors**, all of them, placed as Windows has your displays arranged (Settings >
  Display): side by side where they touch, one above the other where one sits on top, each
  its real size, the side ones turned towards you. Hover one for its name, resolution,
  refresh rate and size.
- **Turn things on the desk:** scroll while dragging something, or click it and use Turn
  left / Turn right / Quarter turn / Straight under the view.
- **Fans per position:** Fans and cooling now has a count for each place (front, top, back,
  bottom) with - and +, so 3 in front, 2 on top and 1 at the back is a few clicks.
- **AIO with RGB:** the pump head can have RGB; chained on the ARGB header, it shows the
  fans' lighting.
- **Wind** instead of dots for the airflow: streaks that run from the intakes through the
  case to the exhausts.

## Fixed

- The case was drawn mirrored (front fans facing the wall once turned round). It's now built
  as real cases are: seen from the front, the glass on the left and the motherboard on the
  right. The view opens looking in through the glass.
- The keyboard lost keys at some angles; it shows every key from any angle now (and parts
  sitting on the board, card or shroud likewise).
