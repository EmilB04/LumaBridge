> **Pre-release.** Eight more games recognised for Logitech LIGHTSYNC.

## New

- **More Logitech LIGHTSYNC games:** Civilization VI, Total War: WARHAMMER III, Terraria,
  Assetto Corsa, iRacing, Grand Theft Auto V, Factorio and American Truck Simulator now
  show up on the Games List with their official LIGHTSYNC integration recognised, the same
  way as Euro Truck Simulator 2 and The Sims 4 already did: with Logitech LIGHTSYNC set up
  on the Integrations page, LumaBridge catches their lighting and shows it on every device;
  otherwise it hands your Logitech gear to the game while it runs.
