> **Pre-release.** Three more built-in games: Dota 2, League of Legends and Forza.

## New

- **Dota 2**, through Valve's Game State Integration (like CS2): your team's color, dimmer at
  night, low health, stuns and hexes, kills, death, and victory or defeat. Set it up once on
  its page in the Games List, and add `-gamestateintegration` to Dota 2's launch options in
  Steam.
- **League of Legends**, through Riot's own Live Client Data API: your side's color, low
  health, death, your kills and multikills, your team's dragons (in the dragon's color),
  barons and heralds, and victory or defeat. Nothing to set up.
- **Forza Horizon 4 / 5 and Forza Motorsport**, through the games' Data Out telemetry: rev
  lights from blue through green, yellow and red, and a red flash at the limiter. Switch on
  Data Out in the game's settings (its page in LumaBridge says how, and has the port).

Not yet tried in a real match - feedback welcome.
