> **Pre-release.** Every device in step (CS2's bomb beats on all of them at once), devices that can sleep, and fixes for the Azoth and Rocket League.

## New

- **Every device in step.** Fans, board, memory, keyboard and mouse now share one clock for
  each effect, so a flash or a wave shows the same moment everywhere. During a game's
  lighting the Logitech mouse follows LED by LED, with a new frame every 20 ms (it used to
  lag behind the others).
- **CS2: the bomb's beat.** Once the bomb is planted, every device flashes red at the same
  moment, from one beat a second to five as the 40 s fuse runs out.
- **Devices can sleep.** When you haven't used your Logitech mouse or ROG Azoth for a while,
  its lighting fades out and LumaBridge stops sending, so it can sleep (and save its
  battery); it lights up again as soon as you use it. The mouse follows G HUB's "turn off
  lighting on inactivity"; the delay is on each device's page (1 minute for the mouse, 5 for
  the keyboard by default).

## Fixed

- **ROG Azoth through the Omni receiver:** 1, Q, S, X and space showed a different color than
  the other keys (they lost their blue). The keyboard also updates twice as often wirelessly.
- **Rocket League:** the lights showed both team colors, mostly blue, whatever your team. Now
  they show your team's color.
- **CS2:** the bomb's blinking restarted on every update, so it looked stuck on and out of
  step between devices.
