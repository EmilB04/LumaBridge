> **Pre-release.** First version that should actually light your Aura hardware.

## New: direct USB control of the motherboard's Aura controller

ASUS's Aura SDK reports no devices on current Armoury Crate versions and could crash
LumaBridge, so LumaBridge now talks to the motherboard's **AURA LED Controller** over
USB, the way `Run-HardwareTest.ps1` did successfully:

- **Motherboard LEDs** and **ARGB header** (e.g. fans on a hub) show up on the **Devices**
  page and follow Manual mode and games.
- The ASUS SDK is no longer used, which removes the crash when resuming lighting control.
  (`[Aura] Backend=sdk` brings it back for older setups.)
- LumaBridge re-sends colors every second so ASUS's lighting service can't take over
  mid-game.

**Not supported:** RAM lighting (not reachable over USB). The Azoth keyboard is next.

## Changed

- "When no game is running → **Release to Armoury Crate**": LumaBridge stops sending
  colors, and the lights keep their last color until Armoury Crate applies its lighting
  again (for example when you open it). Choose **My manual color** to always have
  something showing.
- Switched-off devices on the Devices page stay dark while LumaBridge controls the lights.

## Also included

- `Run-HardwareTest.ps1`: a guided hardware test that produces one report to share.

## Try it

1. Unzip and start `LumaBridge.exe`. If it shows "Lighting control paused", click
   **Resume lighting control**. That's safe now.
2. Switch to **Manual** and pick a color. The motherboard and fans should follow.
3. **Devices** should list "Motherboard LEDs" and "ARGB header (fans, strips)".
