> **Pre-release.** Lighting per device with a drag-and-drop preview of your setup, and the Logitech G502 X Plus lit LED by LED.

## New

- **Lighting per device.** Lighting → Manual now opens with **Your setup**: your fans,
  motherboard, memory, keyboard and mouse, each drawn with its live effect.
  - Drag them to where they are on your desk; every fan moves on its own. The layout is
    saved, and **Reset layout** puts it back.
  - Click a device, or pick it under the preview, and choose **Its own lighting** to give it
    its own effect, colors and speed. The others follow the main lighting.
  - Per-device lighting shows in Manual mode and in Auto mode's "your color" between games.
    Games still light every device alike.
  - The Auto page shows the same preview.
- **Logitech G502 X Plus: the whole effect, LED by LED.** Until now the mouse showed one
  color. LumaBridge now talks to it directly through its receiver, the way G HUB does:
  - breathing, color cycle and the classic rainbow wave run on the mouse itself, smoothly;
  - every other effect (Fire, Ocean and the other rainbows, gradients, comets, twinkle,
    static colors, games) is drawn along its light strip, LED by LED.

  Other Logitech mice still show one color through G HUB.
- **ROG Azoth: Armoury Crate's effects decoded** (static, breathing, color cycle, reactive,
  wave, ripple, starry night, quicksand, current, rain drop). Nothing uses them yet; they're
  the groundwork for letting the keyboard run its own effects.
- **Tools:** `tools\hidpp-probe.exe` lists a Logitech mouse's lighting features, plays its own
  effects (`--test`) and maps its LEDs (`--map`, a step-by-step guide).
  `tools\logiled-harness.exe --zones` tests G HUB's lighting zones.
