> **Pre-release.** Battlefield's own lighting on your Logitech mouse, a choice to keep Logitech gear with LumaBridge during games, and Screen colors on HDR screens.

## New

- **Battlefield lights your Logitech gear itself.** While Battlefield 1, V, 2042 or 6 runs,
  LumaBridge hands your Logitech gear to the game, so the mouse shows Battlefield's own
  LIGHTSYNC effects through G HUB. Your other devices show your choice for "When no game is
  running", or the screen's colors if you pick Screen colors on the game's page. The
  dashboard says so: "Battlefield 2042 - lights Logitech gear through G HUB".
- **Keep Logitech gear with LumaBridge during games.** A new switch on the Logitech device
  page keeps your Logitech gear following LumaBridge even while a game lights it itself. Off
  by default.
- **Screen colors on HDR screens,** and a clear note on the dashboard and the game's page
  when the screen can't be read (a game hidden from capture, or exclusive fullscreen).
- **New tools:** `hidpp-probe --listen` logs what a Logitech mouse reports back, and
  `chroma-probe` checks whether Razer Synapse can pass games' Chroma lighting on to
  LumaBridge.

## Changed

- Battlefield no longer switches to Screen colors by itself.
