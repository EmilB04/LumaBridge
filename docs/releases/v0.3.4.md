> **Pre-release.** A visible countdown whenever the lights go back to Armoury Crate.

## New

- **Hand-back countdown everywhere.** Handing the lights back to Armoury Crate takes about
  5 seconds (the lighting controller restarts and loads Armoury Crate's effect), and until
  now nothing visible happened in that time. A banner with a countdown and a progress bar
  now appears at the top of every page, and the sidebar shows "Handing back to Armoury
  Crate... 5 s". It appears whenever LumaBridge hands back:
  - switching to **Auto** while "When no game is running" is set to **Armoury Crate**,
  - choosing **Armoury Crate** there while in Auto,
  - a game closing in Auto with that setting,
  - **Stop controlling the lights** (Settings).
  The countdown disappears as soon as LumaBridge takes the lights again (e.g. you switch
  back to Manual).
