> **Pre-release.** The processor in Power and clocks.

## Fixed

- **Power and clocks now shows the processor**, not only the graphics card:
  - its **clock**, from Windows' own performance counters (what Task Manager shows), with
    nothing to set up;
  - its **package power**, read by LumaBridge's hardware helper from the processor's energy
    counter (AMD Ryzen and Intel).
- The graphics card's **clock** is shown too (NVIDIA).
- **Intel processors** get their temperature and power through Hardware access as well
  (PawnIO's signed IntelMSR module is now bundled, unmodified).

LumaBridge asks you to **update Hardware access** once (Devices > Hardware access, one
administrator approval), so the helper that reads the processor's power is installed.
