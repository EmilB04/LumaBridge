> **Pre-release.** The ROG Azoth key by key wirelessly too, the app's icon in the window, and a loading screen.

## New

- **ROG Azoth: every key its own color through the Omni receiver too.** Wirelessly, effects
  now run across the keys just like by cable (up to about 10 updates a second over the
  receiver, to spare the battery; about 25 by cable).
- **The app's icon in the window.** The logo in the sidebar is now LumaBridge's own icon.
- **A loading screen** when the window opens: the icon with a ring of colors turning around
  it, fading into the app.
