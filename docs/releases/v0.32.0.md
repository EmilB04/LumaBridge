> **Pre-release.** The controller page shows a live 3D controller with its lightbar color, fast-changing numbers sit behind switches, and the Lighting page shows your manual color.

## New

- **Live 3D controller:** the controller page draws your DualSense in 3D and follows it
  live: held buttons light up and sink, the sticks lean the way you push them, the
  triggers travel as you squeeze and fingers show on the touchpad. Drag to turn it, scroll
  to zoom, double-click to reset. A DualShock 4 is drawn as a DualSense.
- **Lightbar color:** the lightbar's current color, with its hex and RGB values, under the
  model (and on the model itself).
- **Your manual color on the Lighting page:** in Auto mode with "My manual color" as the
  choice between games, a card shows that saved look, with a button to switch to Manual.

## Changed

- **Fast-changing numbers behind switches:** raw stick, trigger and motion readings, and
  report timing, each have their own switch, off by default and remembered. When on, the
  numbers refresh a few times a second so they can be read.
- **Lighting page:** the setup preview can be expanded in either mode.
