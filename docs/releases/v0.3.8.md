> **Pre-release.** The ASUS ROG Azoth follows LumaBridge over its cable (experimental).

## New

- **ASUS ROG Azoth keyboard (wired, experimental).** The whole keyboard shows LumaBridge's
  color (animated effects included), sent over the USB cable using the exact static-color
  command Armoury Crate sends. That command was captured from Armoury Crate on your Azoth.
  - LumaBridge **never** sends Armoury Crate's save command, so nothing is written to the
    keyboard's flash and your saved Armoury Crate lighting stays stored in it.
  - Turn it on under Devices → **Keyboard & mouse** → **Light the ROG Azoth (wired)**. It's
    off by default. The dashboard's Aura devices card shows its state.
  - Not yet: wireless (Omni receiver), per-key colors, and handing back without a restart.
    When LumaBridge lets go, the keyboard keeps the last color until it's unplugged or
    Armoury Crate sets it again.
- Protocol notes: [docs/peripherals.md](https://github.com/EmilB04/LumaBridge/blob/main/docs/peripherals.md).
