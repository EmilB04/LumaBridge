> **Pre-release.** Many more games light up (racing sims built in, about 460 games known by the lighting SDK they speak), Armoury Crate no longer darkens the fans when a USB device is unplugged, and the ROG Azoth's screen gets a GIF collection.

## New

- **More built-in game lighting:** iRacing (your car's shift lights, flags and pit limiter),
  Assetto Corsa, Assetto Corsa Competizione and Assetto Corsa EVO, RaceRoom, EA SPORTS WRC
  (one click sets up its telemetry), WRC Generations, Live for Speed, DiRT 4, GRID and GRID
  Legends, F1 2015 to F1 2018 and the first Project CARS, next to the games that were already
  built in. None of these were checked against the running games yet.
- **About 460 more games known:** the Games List shows which lighting SDK a game speaks
  (Razer Chroma, Logitech LIGHTSYNC, Corsair iCUE, SteelSeries GameSense, Alienware AlienFX)
  before you ever play it, from the vendors' own lists (Razer's included, with recent titles
  such as Control Resonant and 007 First Light). Games that light ASUS Aura
  themselves (Tropico 6, Serious Sam 4, ...) are marked too, and VALORANT points to Screen
  colors.
- **Armoury Crate stays out of the way:** Armoury Crate puts its own lighting back whenever a
  USB device is plugged in or out, so the fans went dark for several seconds and the memory's
  effect stalled. LumaBridge now pauses Armoury Crate's lighting service while it has the
  lights, and starts it again when it hands them back, exits, or while Armoury Crate's
  window is open (**Settings > Armoury Crate**). Needs **Hardware access > Update** once.
  Without it, LumaBridge takes the lights back within a second.
- **ROG Azoth OLED GIFs (test):** six LumaBridge animations (Wave, Level bars, Stars,
  Scanner, Rings, Rain) as live tiles, exported as 256 x 64 GIFs for Armoury Crate's
  upload, with an illustrated four-step guide. The ASUS built-in animations preview with
  Armoury Crate's own artwork.

## Changed

- **ROG Azoth OLED:** LumaBridge always runs the screen; the Armoury Crate / LumaBridge
  choice is gone. **Keep current** leaves what the screen shows to Armoury Crate.
- **F1:** F1 2018 to F1 25 (was F1 24 / F1 25); Forza Motorsport 7 and the 2023 Forza
  Motorsport are recognised by their exe.
- **Games outside the store libraries** with built-in lighting (iRacing, Live for Speed,
  X-Plane, DCS) are found by their exe.
- **The Games List and dashboard** look games up instantly, however many are known.

## Fixed

- **Automobilista 2 / Project CARS:** the games broadcast their telemetry, which LumaBridge
  never received on 127.0.0.1; it now listens for the broadcast (Windows may ask once to
  allow it on the network).
- **Log size:** a lighting device that never takes colors (G HUB's virtual one for a
  Logitech mouse) is tried again quietly every 15 s instead of two log lines every 3 s.
