> **Pre-release.** Built-in lighting for Counter-Strike 2, Rocket League and War Thunder,
> Screen colors for games without lighting, and the version number in the app.

## New

- **Counter-Strike 2** (Valve Game State Integration). Team colors, freeze time, low
  health, flashbangs, fire, the bomb (blinking faster as the fuse runs down), explosions
  and defuses, kills, headshots and round results. Set up once on **Integrations** → Built-in
  game feeds → Counter-Strike 2, then restart CS2.
- **Rocket League** (Psyonix Stats API). Both team colors around the fans, a burst in the
  scoring team's color on every goal, overtime, and the winner. Switch it on once on
  **Integrations**, then restart Rocket League.
- **War Thunder** (the game's local status page). Tank crew health from green to red, with
  a flash on crew loss. Aircraft: war emergency power and low fuel. Nothing to set up.
- These feeds use official data the games publish on your PC. Nothing is added to the game,
  so anti-cheat isn't involved.
- **Screen colors:** games without dynamic lighting can show the screen's colors on the fans
  and board (left and right of the screen, blended around each fan). Turn it on for all such
  games on the Lighting page (Auto), or per game on the Games List ("Without game
  lighting": Default / Screen colors / My idle choice). It only sees the screen image, never
  the game.
- **Known games:**
  - Overwatch 2 (Razer Chroma) and Battlefield 1 / 2042 / V (Logitech / Razer, blocked by
    EA's anti-cheat) show what applies to them.
  - Battlefield 6, BOMBANANA!, NO BACKUP and RV There Yet? are marked as having no lighting
    support.
  - DSX is recognized as a tool and ignored.
- **Version number** in the bottom-left corner and in Settings → About.

## Changed

- The Armoury Crate hand-back countdown is now **10 seconds**, to match how long the
  controller really takes to restart and load Armoury Crate's effect.
