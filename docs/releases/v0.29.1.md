> **Pre-release.** DualSense Bluetooth lighting fixes and corrected monitor supports in the 3D preview.

## Fixed

- **DualSense over Bluetooth:** detect the controller when Windows reports a larger HID
  collection, use the correct Bluetooth header, color offsets and checksum, and release
  the startup lights before applying LumaBridge's color. Lightbar changes are confirmed
  on a real wireless controller.
- **Bluetooth handoff:** initialize the lightbar again when LumaBridge takes lighting
  control back. The status shows the brief startup wait, and write failures remain visible
  in the device card and log.
- **Monitor stands:** place the horizontal monitor's support behind the back cover instead
  of inside the screen. Vertical monitors have no stand or support arm; small desk panels
  continue to rest without a stand.
