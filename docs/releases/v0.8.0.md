> **Pre-release.** A Devices page with a page per device, a preview that draws your own board and memory, and brightness per device.

## New

- **Devices, one page each.** The Devices page is now a list like the Games List: every
  device with its status (Aura devices, the memory, Logitech devices, the ROG Azoth). Click
  one for its own page:
  - its settings: the fan layout and test pattern on the fans' page, the RAM options and
    hardware access on the memory's, switching an Aura device on or off;
  - its lighting, with **Change its lighting** to go straight to it on the Lighting page.
- **Brightness per device.** Pick a device on the Lighting page and its Brightness slider
  dims just that device, on top of the overall brightness. It applies in games too.
- **The preview draws your own hardware.** LumaBridge reads your board and memory from the
  PC's firmware tables and draws them to match:
  - ASUS ROG / ROG Strix and TUF boards with their lit logo, labelled with the model;
  - the memory in the board's slots, in the slots it's really in, each stick lit top to
    bottom, shaped like Kingston FURY or HyperX FURY sticks;
  - the ROG Azoth with its real layout: F-row, knob and screen, arrows.

## Fixed

- **The mouse ignored the brightness.** It now dims with it, including the mouse's own
  breathing, cycle and wave.
- **Brightness under a selected device moved every device.** It now sets that device's own
  brightness.
