> **Pre-release.** Rocket League's team colors stay vivid, even for muted custom colors.

## Fixed

- **Rocket League colors:** a team color that's muted, dark or close to gray (Rocket League
  lets players pick almost any primary color in the game's settings) is boosted to a clear,
  saturated hue before it reaches your lights. Already-vivid colors are untouched. Applies
  everywhere the team color shows: the ambient gradient, goal flashes and comets, and the
  winner's breathing at the end. **Not checked in the running app** (no Windows build
  available this round) - the native, portable unit tests (`test_core`) cover the boost and
  the untouched-when-vivid case and pass.
