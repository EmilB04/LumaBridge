> **Pre-release.** My setup moves better: smooth airflow, a view you can slide around, and
> small screens where they really stand. NZXT Krakens are found more reliably.

## New

- **Slide the view:** right-drag, middle-drag, Shift-drag or the arrow keys move the view
  across the desk, so things at the sides are easy to reach. Double-click something to bring
  it to the middle; double-click the desk to start over.
- **NZXT Kraken on Integrations:** a card with the Kraken LumaBridge found, whether it's
  reading it, and its liquid temperature and pump and fan speeds.
- **Kraken 2024 Plus** is recognised, and newer screen Krakens are too (as "NZXT Kraken").
  If an NZXT device isn't recognised, Fans and cooling shows its USB product number.

## Fixed

- **Airflow:** the wind streaks jumped whenever a fan speed changed, and visibly restarted
  on the same path. They now move smoothly, ease to new fan speeds, fade in and out at both
  ends, and take a new path each time.
- **Small screens under a monitor:** a small screen (under 16") that sits below a monitor in
  Windows' display layout now stands on the desk right beneath it, with the monitor above
  sitting just over it, with no gap. Other small screens still rest on the desk in front.
- **Moving things:** dragged things now follow the mouse one to one (they were grabbed at
  desk level, so they ran ahead of the mouse, most of all towards the sides).
- **Kraken reading:** LumaBridge now tries each of the Kraken's USB interfaces until one
  answers, and says what's happening (connecting, no reply, can't open) instead of staying
  silent. The log lists the interfaces it found.
